# Finite reconstruction plan from the mark

Build one replayable route from `<>` through admission, separation, obstruction and structure to the existing finite quantum examples. Get the route working with named provisional inputs; then remove inputs, substitute weaker ones, and measure what breaks. Gemini's exploratory work remains available for integration when its inputs and outputs match a demonstrated part of this route.

This is a proposal for successive small slices. It does not authorize all of them at once. The first implementation slice is specified below for discussion under [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).

## The starting point already exists

WDP's [Genesis construction][construction] records literal `Supply("<>")`, calls the existing admission implementation, compares each accepted prefix with WDP, and constructs a finite triangle and its filling. It retains the complete admission state and history alongside the mathematical interpretation. Its reported cycle has rational Betti numbers `(1,1)`; the filled triangle has `(1,0,0)`. Filling changes the old homology answer, and the record says so.

Its [Lean companion][construction-lean] already proves that accepted replay has a typed history with exactly the supplied events, and that a commuting interpretation transports finite replay, including refusal. These are the tools for our connection to Omega. We should not rebuild either the root walk or the generic history theorem.

Omega's current local and remote main is `3ba94e1e455295da278c855c434f0b3ba83b520a`, including merged PR #7. It already contains admission/separation, quotient descent, retention/refusal, local/global obstruction, and the latest two-sheet holonomy classification. Its [DAG](../../dag/derivation_dag.json) keeps the endogenous invocation and holonomy-selection obligations open. Those exploratory results can stay in their current branch of the mathematics while we connect the earlier route.

The WDP sources below are pinned to TFQM `1a7263e924accae0e114ab258b1747a84f693080`; Genesis Ongoing is pinned to `e38945d146faaa2c60dcae96cb6b6c6c5123bf38`. Education notes sometimes describe older revisions. Their historical theorem counts are not current build results. Concurrent uncommitted edits appeared in Omega's obstruction/profile modules, DAG and tests during this planning pass; they were left untouched and are outside this snapshot review.

## Repeated arguments and genuine connections

**One reusable obstruction:** if a report identifies two cases whose required answers differ, no decoder of that report can answer both correctly. Genesis observational collapse, Omega's mirror/quotient results, WDP report descent, and phase-blind interference instantiate this argument. Preserve their different reports and tasks; do not count each instance as a new foundation.

**One reusable replay construction:** accepted partial transitions generate typed histories. Event extraction and replay recover the endpoint. WDP now supplies both directions and transport through a commuting interpretation. The remaining work is to establish the particular interpretation between our implementations.

**One concrete algebraic bridge:** H3 source 23's [raising theorem][raising] changes pair excess by `f(u)-f(v)`. That connects its actual operation to a coboundary calculation. The raising rule and two-relation count language are inputs. They are not obtained by renaming the mark.

**Distinct obligations:** a current report can preserve an answer and still lose information needed by a future edit. WDP already gives this example: two raising-equivalent representatives can differ after adding a term and filling a triple. Keep the raw representative/history when that continuation is protected. Likewise, filling a cycle and preserving its old homology are different tasks.

## First implementation slice for agreement

**Task:** connect Omega's existing successful and refused `Item4` moves to WDP's existing typed replay, while retaining the literal-root/admission prefix as an explicit source trace.

**Sources:** Omega [MinimalSeparation](../../lean/MinimalSeparation.lean), especially `applyItem4`, `applyDelta4`, `R2L4`, `R2F4`, and the fresh-unary refinement; WDP [GenerativeHistoryCompression][histories] and [GenesisConstruction][construction-lean]. The existing root construction supplies the starting example.

**Supplied assumptions:** host Lean logic/equality; the existing finite regime fields; positive-arity form grammar; ordered events; the L/F admission choice; the separation requirement; and item/arity price. Source tokens, repeated-admission behavior and rule fields must be accounted for at the interface. `Item4` has no supply or impose constructor: do not silently add one or claim it already generates the whole prefix. Restrict the proven interface to its declared common fragment and show the explicit initialization boundary.

**Intended result:** instantiate the existing typed-history construction with `applyItem4`; establish that WDP replay equals Omega's `applyDelta4`; derive both accepted-history directions using the existing theorems. Where an actual source/target state and event translation is used, prove its partial-step equation, including `none`. Preserve event order, chosen cost and the selected distinguishing answers. Successful histories and refused attempts remain distinguishable records.

**Checks:**

1. Literal supply leaves the source admitted state empty but remains in the trace; admitting it is a different event.
2. L and F reach their own explicit initial comparison states. The initialization is displayed, not inferred from the glyph.
3. Declare a unary form then assert it at either target: separation succeeds at the declared two-item price. Keep both labeled alternatives.
4. Reverse the two events: undeclared-form use refuses. Zero arity, wrong arity and out-of-range arguments follow the selected source semantics.
5. Extract and replay accepted events; obtain the exact endpoint. Concatenation preserves order and the selected additive event price.
6. Duplicate accepted instances can leave the endpoint unchanged while adding a history occurrence. Endpoint-only comparison must fail when occurrence count is protected.
7. Retain the existing re-collapse counterexample and the fresh-form preservation control. A history wrapper must not turn one into the other.
8. Mutate event order, an argument, an expected refusal and a retained report; each relevant checker must reject the corrupted claim.

Use Omega's Python environment and pinned Lean 4.33.0. Build only the selected adapter and minimal reused core dependencies. WDP's existing construction receipt used 4.33.1, so compatibility must actually be checked. No legacy-project build, Cargo rebuild or dependency download is needed to approve this proposal.

The first gap is therefore a specific transition/replay correspondence in Omega, not a missing theory of histories. The generic replay lemmas alone do not establish equivalence with either Rust implementation. The broader source correspondence remains a later obligation unless this slice proves it on its stated fragment.

## Test whether continuation becomes self-maintaining

Start with H3's existing feedback branch. Its [unbounded-history theorem][feedback] inserts arbitrarily many two-step cycles into an existing path. That provides a useful control: activity without new structure.

For each finite run, fix the initial admissions, legal moves, protected questions, candidate ordering and resource bound. Record the origin of every next demand: retained state, an admitted rule, or a fresh supplied input. A scheduler that calls the next transition is itself part of the experiment.

Compare feedback on/off and several explicitly named tie policies. Measure preserved distinctions, new obligations, discharge, repeated states and retained history. A run that continues without fresh input is a result under that fixed policy. Stronger claims require showing which policy choices can vary without changing the claimed behavior. This is where we investigate self-maintenance rather than assigning it to a lift number.

## Thin the working route

For every working slice, hold its protected tasks fixed and remove or weaken one admission. Keep the counterexample when behavior changes. If two formulations translate both ways and preserve the required operations and answers, count them as alternative presentations of the same work. If they agree only at the present endpoint, retain the difference.

Report costs separately: admitted data, forms, instances, persistent requirements, history storage and execution budget. A minimum names its comparison class and cost. Prefer the smaller successful package within that comparison; do not call an unsearched package globally minimal.

Each completed slice should leave one runnable example, its explicit inputs, exact output, theorem/axiom report, targeted adversarial controls and the next unresolved interface. New files stay in Omega. Historical evidence and the older [finite route snapshot](2026-11-06-finite-route.json) remain unchanged.
