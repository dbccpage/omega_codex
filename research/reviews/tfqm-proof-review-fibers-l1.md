## Independent finite checks

An independent Python enumeration tested 15,625 pairs of integer triangle cochains and potentials in `[-2,2]^3`: every residual norm respected the face lower bound. For all 125 cochains in `[-2,2]^3`, a potential in `[-4,4]^3` attained it. This is bounded corroboration, not a replacement for Lean's unbounded theorem.

An independent enumeration of the 24 permutations of `Bool × Bool` found exactly four whose first output is the stated constant collapse on both inputs with the prepared second bit; in each, the hidden output distinguishes the two prepared inputs. This checks that finite combinatorial claim for the specified model, not its physical interpretation.

The prior import-wrapper comparison and its caveats are in `tfqm-review-progress.md`. The overlapping triangle constructions here use closely related coordinate definitions; equality of full files or of their dependency graphs was not established.
